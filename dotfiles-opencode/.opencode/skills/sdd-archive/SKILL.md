# SDD Archive Skill

## Objective

Close a completed change by recording final artifacts and lessons learned.

## Workflow

1. Confirm verify phase is complete.
2. Summarize delivered scope and deviations.
3. Record final artifact links/locations.
4. Capture follow-up items and technical debt.

## Output Format

- `status`
- `executive_summary`
- `archive_record`
  - Delivered
  - Not Delivered
  - Evidence
  - Follow-ups
- `next_recommended`
- `risks`

## Guardrails

- Do not archive changes that failed verification.
- Keep final record concise and auditable.
